# 01-Inbox: the single entry point

- Anything new — ideas, clippings, links — **lands here first, unclassified**. Say `/intake/collect <content>` to the AI, or drop files in yourself.
- Classification happens during the weekly cleanup, not at capture time (keep capture friction low).
- Weekly triage routes everything to one of three places: `03-Projects/<p>/10-source/` (project material), `02-Areas/<area>/` (judgments) or `04-Resources/clippings/` (originals) — or the trash.
