# Brief: demo-noteai-course

> AI reads this file at the start of every session. Keep it under 500 words; update when stale.
> This project is a demo — copy, rename, or delete it.

## 1. One-sentence goal
Help 30+ office workers run their first AI content side hustle loop in 4 weeks, leaving able to draft their own topic list immediately.

## 2. Deliverables
Format: 6 illustrated lessons + 1 checklist PDF; 1500–2000 words each; platforms: WeChat official account + paid newsletter.

## 3. Audience
Role: employed, zero-programming office workers; already know: casual AI chat; fears: sounding too "AI-flavored", no time; change after reading: from "bookmarking" to "publishing this week".

## 4. Core angle (vs. typical content)
Most tutorials teach tools; this course teaches the source → draft → final pipeline and the division of labor — tools are just plugins.

## 5. Voice & style
Direct, restrained, self-deprecating humor allowed; short sentences; one concrete example per paragraph. Reference: [[04-Resources/voice-reference]].

## 6. Must include / Must avoid
Include: 1 same-day actionable step per lesson; at least 1 failure story.
Avoid: fabricated data/cases; buzzwords ("empower", "leverage", "disrupt"); income promises; competitor names.

## 7. Sources & facts
- `10-source/interviews/` (interview transcripts, append-only)
- `20-notes/` (summary & case cards)
