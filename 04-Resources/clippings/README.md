# clippings — third-party originals

Third-party articles are stored here verbatim (never rewritten, abridged, or summarized). AI read-only.

- Ingest: `/intake/clip <URL or local file>` (the AI stages into 01-Inbox; you drag the file here), or drop files in manually.
- Naming: `date-source-title.md`; frontmatter keeps `source:` and `captured:`.
- After reading, to distill a judgment → `/knowledge/note`: judgments go to `02-Areas/<area>/` and link back to the original — **never annotate the original itself**.
